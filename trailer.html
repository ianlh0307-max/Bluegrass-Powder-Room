The trailer

A proper powder room for your outdoor wedding or event, with original Kentucky art in every stall and heat and air for every season.

What your guests will find

Original Kentucky art in every stall

Each stall is hung with original work by Kentucky artists. With the Premium package, you choose the art style that suits your day.

Heat and air for every season

The trailer is climate controlled, so guests stay warm at a late-fall barn wedding and cool at a July garden party.

Fresh flowers inside and out

Premium bookings include fresh flowers both inside the trailer and outside it.

Delivered, set up and picked up

We bring the trailer to your venue, set it up before guests arrive, and take it away after the event.

Cleaned and stocked

The trailer arrives cleaned and stocked with supplies for your event.

Choose the art for your day

Premium bookings pick the art style that hangs in the stalls, so the trailer fits the look of your wedding.
